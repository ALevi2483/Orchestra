Become Part of the Orchestra

Learn music, perform with friends, and be part of one of the most rewarding programs at Western Branch Middle School.

Contact the Director

Why Join Orchestra?

Orchestra students build confidence, teamwork, responsibility, and musical skill while participating in concerts and school performances throughout the year.

Students also develop discipline, creativity, leadership, and friendships that often last for years.

No Experience Needed

Beginners are welcome.

Students will learn:

  • How to play a string instrument
  • How to read music
  • Proper instrument care
  • Performance skills
  • Teamwork in an ensemble setting

Instruments Offered

Violin
Viola
Cello
Bass

What Students Need

  • Positive attitude
  • Willingness to practice
  • Instrument and supplies
  • Participation in concerts
  • Respect for fellow musicians

Concerts & Events

Orchestra students participate in:

  • Winter Concerts
  • Spring Concerts
  • School Performances
  • Honor Orchestra Auditions
  • Community Events
  • Festival Performances

How to Join

Students interested in orchestra should speak with the orchestra director or school counselor during course selection.

Parents with questions about instruments, beginner classes, supplies, or scheduling are encouraged to contact the orchestra director.
