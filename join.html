Why Join Orchestra?

Learn an Instrument

Develop musical skills that can last a lifetime while learning teamwork, discipline, and creativity.

Perform

Participate in concerts, festivals, school events, and opportunities throughout the year.

Be Part of a Team

Build friendships and work together with other students to create something special.

No Experience Needed

Beginners are welcome. Orchestra classes are designed to teach students everything they need to know from the ground up.

Students learn instrument care, music reading, performance skills, and ensemble playing in a supportive environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own instrument?

Many students rent instruments from local music stores. School-owned instruments may also be available in limited quantities.

How much does orchestra cost?

Costs vary depending on instrument rental and supplies. Contact the director for current information.

Can I participate in sports and orchestra?

Yes. Many students successfully participate in orchestra along with sports and other extracurricular activities.

New Student Checklist

  • Select orchestra during course registration.
  • Choose an instrument.
  • Obtain required supplies.
  • Attend rehearsals and concerts.
  • Practice consistently.
  • Have fun making music.

Ready to Join?

Students interested in orchestra should speak with the orchestra director or their school counselor during course selection.

Parents with questions about instruments, scheduling, supplies, or enrollment are encouraged to contact the director.
