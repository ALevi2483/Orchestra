Student Basics

Practice & Instrument Care

A few simple habits help students stay prepared for rehearsals and performances.

Practice

  • Practice regularly instead of waiting until the last minute.
  • Use a metronome when appropriate.
  • Practice difficult sections slowly before increasing tempo.
  • Follow assignments and guidance from your orchestra director.

Instrument Care

  • Keep your instrument in its case when not in use.
  • Do not leave your instrument in a hot or cold vehicle.
  • Handle your instrument carefully during transportation.
  • Report damage or problems promptly.

Bow Care

  • Loosen the bow after playing.
  • Avoid touching the bow hair.
  • Store the bow securely in the case.
  • Ask your director before attempting repairs.
