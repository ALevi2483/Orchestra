Instrument Care

Protect your instrument and keep it ready for rehearsals and performances.

Protect Your Instrument

  • Keep your instrument in its case when not in use.
  • Do not leave your instrument in a hot or cold vehicle.
  • Handle your instrument carefully when transporting it.

Bow Care

  • Loosen the bow after playing.
  • Avoid touching the bow hair.
  • Store the bow securely in the case.

Repairs

  • Report damage or broken strings promptly.
  • Do not force pegs or fine tuners.
  • Ask your director before attempting repairs yourself.
