Practice Expectations

Short, focused practice works better than rushing right before a concert. Students should practice at home several times each week.

Beginner

15 to 20 minutes per day

Intermediate

20 to 30 minutes per day

Advanced

30 or more minutes per day

Practice Checklist

  • Tune first.
  • Use a pencil.
  • Start slowly.
  • Work small sections.
  • End with something you can play well.

Instrument Care

Take care of your instrument so it is ready for class and performances.

Every Day

  • Bring your instrument, music, and pencil.
  • Keep your instrument in its case when not playing.
  • Loosen your bow after playing.

Avoid Damage

  • Do not leave instruments in hot or cold vehicles.
  • Do not touch the bow hair.
  • Do not force pegs, strings, or fine tuners.

Get Help

  • Report broken strings quickly.
  • Tell the director about damage right away.
  • Ask before trying to fix something yourself.

Classroom Expectations

Be Prepared

Arrive on time with your instrument, music, pencil, and supplies.

Be Respectful

Respect classmates, instruments, equipment, and rehearsal time.

Be Engaged

Participate, listen, ask questions, and work to improve each rehearsal.
