Rising 6th Grade

Getting Started at Jolliff

Sixth grade orchestra continues the skills students learned in 5th grade strings. Students should locate and keep their Essential Elements book for use in 6th grade orchestra.

  • Violin, viola, and cello continue to be offered.
  • Bass is also available beginning in middle school.
  • Students interested in changing instruments will have an opportunity to audition.
  • Auditions to change instruments will be completed on the instrument the student played in 5th grade.
  • Students who need an instrument for an audition can speak with Mrs. Rosenberg.

No instruments are needed during the first week of school. Mrs. Rosenberg will let students know when they should begin bringing instruments to class.

Instruments

Instrument Sizing

During the first few weeks of school, Mrs. Rosenberg will measure students who do not currently have an instrument or who have a fractional-size instrument.

Students should not automatically move to the next instrument size simply because they have grown since 5th grade.

When a student is between instrument sizes, it is better to use the smaller size until they can be properly measured.

If you are unsure about instrument size, wait until your student can be measured before renting or purchasing a larger instrument.

Jolliff Orchestra

First Week Reminder

Jolliff students have orchestra class beginning on the first day of school, but students should not bring their instruments during the first week.

The first week gives students time to learn classroom procedures, navigate their new school, manage class transitions, and become comfortable with their schedules before adding an instrument to the routine.

Mrs. Rosenberg will tell students when they should begin bringing instruments to class.

Need an Instrument?

Students who do not currently have an instrument should not worry. Mrs. Rosenberg will work with students during the first few weeks of school to determine the proper instrument and size.

If your student still has a fractional-size instrument from 5th grade, have them continue using it until they can be properly measured.
