Rising 6th Grade

Getting Started at Jolliff

Sixth grade orchestra continues the skills students learned in 5th grade strings. Students should locate and keep their Essential Elements book for use in 6th grade orchestra.

  • Violin, viola, and cello continue to be offered.
  • Bass is also available beginning in middle school.
  • Students interested in changing instruments will have an opportunity to audition.
  • Auditions to change instruments will be completed on the instrument the student played in 5th grade.
  • Students who need an instrument for an audition can speak with Mrs. Rosenberg.

Mrs. Rosenberg will let students know when instruments are needed for class and will provide sizing guidance when necessary.

Instruments

Instrument Sizing & Guidelines

Mrs. Rosenberg measures students who do not currently have an instrument or who are using a fractional-size instrument to determine whether they are ready for a different size.

Students should not automatically move to the next instrument size simply because they have grown.

When a student is between instrument sizes, it is better to continue using the smaller size until they can be properly measured.

Before renting or purchasing an instrument, families should review the Jolliff Orchestra instrument guidelines and recommended music store information.

Instrument Guidelines & Music Store List

Why Instrument Quality Matters: "Violin Shaped Objects"

If you are unsure about instrument size or quality, wait until your student can be measured or consult Mrs. Rosenberg before renting or purchasing an instrument.

Jolliff Orchestra

Instrument Quality Matters

A properly sized, playable instrument is important to a student's success in orchestra. Families should use the Jolliff Orchestra instrument guidelines and recommended music store information before renting or purchasing an instrument.

Some inexpensive instruments sold online may be difficult to tune, may not stay in tune, or may have poor sound quality. If you are unsure whether an instrument is appropriate, check with Mrs. Rosenberg before purchasing or renting.

View Instrument Guidelines & Music Store List

Need an Instrument or a Different Size?

Mrs. Rosenberg can help determine the appropriate instrument and size for students who need an instrument or are ready to move from a fractional-size instrument.

Students should continue using their current instrument until they have been properly measured if there is any question about sizing.
