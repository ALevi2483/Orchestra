Student Resources

Practice Expectations

Students are encouraged to practice regularly at home to improve performance skills and preparation for concerts.

  • Beginner Students: 15 to 20 minutes daily
  • Intermediate Students: 20 to 30 minutes daily
  • Advanced Students: 30+ minutes daily

Instrument Care Tips

  • Always loosen your bow after playing.
  • Store instruments in their cases when not in use.
  • Do not leave instruments in hot vehicles.
  • Bring rosin and music to every class.
  • Report damaged strings or equipment quickly.

Audition Information

Honor Orchestra and seating audition information will be posted here throughout the school year.

Students should check this page regularly for updates, audition materials, and deadlines.

Classroom Expectations

  • Arrive on time and prepared.
  • Respect instruments and equipment.
  • Participate during rehearsals.
  • Show respect to fellow musicians.
  • Practice responsibility and teamwork.
