Student Basics

Practice & Instrument Care

Simple habits help keep students and instruments ready for rehearsals and performances.

Practice

  • Practice regularly.
  • Work difficult sections slowly.
  • Use a metronome when appropriate.
  • Follow assignments from your director.

Instrument Care

  • Keep your instrument in its case when not in use.
  • Never leave it in a hot or cold vehicle.
  • Handle and transport it carefully.
  • Report damage promptly.

Bow Care

  • Loosen the bow after playing.
  • Do not touch the bow hair.
  • Store the bow securely in its case.
  • Ask before attempting repairs.
