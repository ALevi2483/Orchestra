About the Orchestra Program

Our Mission

The Western Branch Middle School Orchestra program is dedicated to helping students develop musical skills, confidence, teamwork, and creativity through string education and performance.

Students are encouraged to grow both individually and as members of a larger ensemble while learning responsibility, discipline, and leadership.

Performance Opportunities

Orchestra students participate in:

  • Winter Concerts
  • Spring Concerts
  • School Performances
  • Honor Orchestra Auditions
  • Community Events
  • Festival Performances

Why Orchestra?

Orchestra helps students build:

  • Confidence
  • Teamwork
  • Discipline
  • Listening Skills
  • Creativity
  • Leadership

Music education also supports academic growth and encourages students to become lifelong learners.
