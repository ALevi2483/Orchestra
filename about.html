About the Orchestra Program

Learning, performing, and growing through music.

Who We Are

The Western Branch Middle School Orchestra program provides students with opportunities to learn string instruments, perform in concerts, and develop musical skills that will serve them throughout their education.

Students participate in rehearsals, concerts, special events, and enrichment opportunities throughout the school year while learning the value of preparation, teamwork, and commitment.
