Orchestra Boosters

About the Boosters

The Orchestra Boosters support the orchestra program through fundraising, volunteering, event support, and student encouragement.

Parent and community involvement helps provide additional opportunities and resources for orchestra students throughout the school year.

What Boosters Help Support

  • Concert events
  • Student recognition and awards
  • Festival and trip support
  • Music purchases
  • Classroom supplies
  • Special performances and activities

Volunteer Opportunities

Volunteers are always appreciated.

  • Concert setup and cleanup
  • Photography
  • Fundraising events
  • Chaperoning trips
  • Uniform organization
  • Event planning
