Parent Information

How Parents Can Help

Parent support plays a major role in student success within the orchestra program.

  • Encourage regular practice at home
  • Ensure students arrive prepared for rehearsals
  • Attend concerts and performances
  • Support fundraising efforts
  • Volunteer when opportunities are available

Concert Expectations

  • Students should arrive early for performances.
  • Concert attire is required.
  • Please remain seated during performances.
  • Silence phones during concerts.
  • Encourage students to stay for the entire event.

Supplies Students May Need

  • Instrument and case
  • Rosin
  • Shoulder rest if applicable
  • Extra strings
  • Music folder
  • Pencil for rehearsal markings

Volunteer Opportunities

Parents are welcome to assist with:

  • Concert setup and cleanup
  • Fundraisers
  • Chaperoning events
  • Uniform organization
  • Photography during events

Communication

Important updates may be shared through:

  • Email
  • School announcements
  • Google Classroom
  • The orchestra website calendar

Please check regularly for schedule changes and announcements.
