Western Branch Orchestras Parents Association

About WBOPA

The Western Branch Orchestras Parents Association is a nonprofit organization made up of parent volunteers dedicated to supporting and promoting orchestra programs in the Western Branch community of Chesapeake, Virginia.

WBOPA supports orchestra students and directors at Western Branch High School and Jolliff Middle School throughout their musical journey.

Our Mission

We support and promote our orchestra programs, directors, and students while helping provide opportunities and resources that enhance students' musical education and appreciation of the arts.

What WBOPA Does

Parent and community support helps strengthen both orchestra programs.

Get Involved

WBOPA Meetings

WBOPA meetings are held during the school year and are open to parents and others interested in supporting the orchestra programs.

Meetings are currently held by Zoom on the second Thursday of each month from 7:30 PM to 8:00 PM.

Meeting information and schedule changes will be shared with orchestra families as needed.

Join a Meeting

Parents and families are encouraged to participate. Contact WBOPA for current meeting information and the Zoom link.
