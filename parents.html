Western Branch Orchestras Parents Association

About WBOPA

The Western Branch Orchestras Parents Association is a nonprofit organization made up of parent volunteers who support orchestra students and directors at Western Branch High School and Jolliff Middle School.

WBOPA helps support performances, trips, activities, fundraising, resources, and other opportunities that enhance the orchestra experience for our students.

Parents, families, and community members are encouraged to participate and help support our orchestra programs.

Our Mission

Support and promote our orchestra programs, directors, and students while helping provide resources and opportunities that strengthen students' musical education and appreciation of the arts.

Volunteer With WBOPA

WBOPA depends on parents, families, and community members who volunteer throughout the school year.

You can help with concerts, fundraisers, student activities, events, communications, setup, or other orchestra needs.

Whether you can help throughout the year or with a single event, your time helps support our student musicians.
