Western Branch Orchestras Parents Association

About WBOPA

The Western Branch Orchestras Parents Association is a nonprofit organization made up of parent volunteers dedicated to supporting and promoting orchestra programs in the Western Branch community of Chesapeake, Virginia.

WBOPA supports orchestra students and directors at Western Branch High School and Jolliff Middle School throughout their musical journey.

Our Mission

We support and promote our orchestra programs, directors, and students while helping provide opportunities and resources that enhance students' musical education and appreciation of the arts.

What We Support

Fundraising and parent involvement help WBOPA support both orchestra programs.

Students & Directors

WBOPA works with our orchestra directors and families to provide resources and support for orchestra students.

Instruments & Resources

WBOPA helps provide instruments, supplies, and other resources needed by the orchestra programs.

Student Opportunities

WBOPA supports performances, trips, scholarships, activities, and other opportunities that extend students' musical experiences.

Get Involved

WBOPA Meetings

WBOPA meetings are open to parents and others interested in supporting the Western Branch orchestra programs.

Meetings are currently held by Zoom on the second Thursday of each month from 7:30 PM to 8:00 PM.

Meeting information and schedule changes will be shared with orchestra families as needed.

Join a Meeting

Parents and families are encouraged to participate. Contact WBOPA for the current Zoom meeting information.

Help Support Our Orchestras

WBOPA depends on parents, families, and community members who volunteer their time throughout the school year.

Opportunities may include concerts, fundraisers, student activities, events, communications, setup, and other orchestra needs.

You do not need to commit a large amount of time. Helping with one event or activity still makes a difference.
