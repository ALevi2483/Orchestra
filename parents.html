Western Branch Orchestras Parents Association

About WBOPA

The Western Branch Orchestras Parents Association is a nonprofit association made up of parent volunteers who support orchestra students and directors at Western Branch High School and Jolliff Middle School.

WBOPA supports performances, trips, activities, fundraising, resources, and other opportunities that enhance the orchestra experience for students.

Parents, families, and community members are encouraged to participate and help support our orchestra programs.

Our Mission

Support and promote our orchestra programs, directors, and students while helping provide resources and opportunities that strengthen students' musical education and appreciation of the arts.
