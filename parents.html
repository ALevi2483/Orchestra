How Parents Can Help

Parent support makes a real difference. Students do better when families help them stay organized, encourage practice, and attend performances.

  • Encourage regular practice at home.
  • Help students remember instruments, music, and supplies.
  • Check the orchestra calendar for updates.
  • Attend concerts and school performances.
  • Volunteer when help is needed.

Best Parent Habit

Check the calendar weekly. Most confusion comes from missed dates, changed rehearsal times, or concert reminders.

Concert Expectations

Help students arrive prepared and ready to perform.

Student Arrival

Students should arrive 30 minutes before the concert start time unless a different report time is announced.

Concert Attire

Students must wear required concert attire for performances. Attire details will be shared before each concert.

Audience Etiquette

Please silence phones, remain seated during performances, and stay for the full event when possible.

Student Supplies

Students should bring required materials to class and rehearsals.

Required

  • Instrument and case
  • Music folder
  • Pencil
  • Assigned music

Instrument Supplies

  • Rosin
  • Shoulder rest, if needed
  • Cleaning cloth
  • Extra strings, if recommended

Good Habits

  • Label cases and supplies.
  • Keep music organized.
  • Report damage quickly.
  • Do not leave instruments in vehicles.
