Frequently Asked Questions

Does my student need experience?

No. Beginner students are welcome. Orchestra classes are designed to teach students how to read music and play their instrument.

What instruments are offered?

Students may learn:

  • Violin
  • Viola
  • Cello
  • Bass

Does my student need to own an instrument?

No. Many students rent instruments from local music stores. School-owned instruments may also be available in limited numbers.

Are concerts required?

Yes. Concert performances are part of the orchestra curriculum and are considered an important part of the student experience.

How often should students practice?

Students should practice regularly at home.

  • Beginners: 15 to 20 minutes daily
  • Intermediate students: 20 to 30 minutes daily
  • Advanced students: 30+ minutes daily

What should students bring to class?

  • Instrument
  • Bow
  • Rosin
  • Music folder
  • Pencil
  • Required music

What should students wear to concerts?

Concert attire information will be shared before performances. Typical attire includes black pants or skirt, white shirt, and black dress shoes.

Can students participate in sports and orchestra?

Yes. Many students successfully participate in orchestra along with sports and other extracurricular activities.

Who should I contact with questions?

Parents and students can contact the orchestra director through the information listed on the Contact page.
